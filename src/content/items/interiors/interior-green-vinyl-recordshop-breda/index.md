The design concept for this shop was cool, chilled and ambient. I started the process by designing the racks for the turntables, the shelves sit at about a 35 degree angle so the turntables can be seen easily. The rest of the interior is constructed from thick scaffolding planks. Every aspect was designed around efficiency and usability. Costs where kept to a minimum by not painting the wood as it has a wonderful texture of its own.
