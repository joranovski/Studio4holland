The inspiration for this deck came from all those 70's and 80's Scifi space movies, around the workshop we called it the mars lander. Needles to say it is very heavy and due to its shape does not pick up vibration from the loudspeakers. as an extra measure against Vibration it is now fitted with our Hextech feet technology. (not shown in these photos).

The drive is of course from the legendary technics SL 1200 - control is all bespoke made buttons - arm is a modified SL 1200 arm pod unit.
